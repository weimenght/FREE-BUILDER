How I use AI

Almost everything on this site was built with AI as a collaborator. A person still decides what gets made, why, and what goes out. Here is what that means in practice.

01

What AI does here

It writes and checks code, drafts the five-language translations, helps sketch games and pages, and suggests wording. It makes small things possible for one person to build.

02

What stays human

What to build and for whom, every learning-design choice, and the final say on what is published. I test what AI drafts before it goes live, and I change or cut what doesn't hold up.

03

Your words don't go to an AI

None of the tools or games on this site sends what you type to an AI service. Nothing leaves your device unless you allow it, and nothing is sold, tracked or analysed.

04

People talk to people

AI can be a warm-up, a practice partner or a first draft. It never replaces a colleague, a coach or a manager's judgement. Every activity is designed to end in a real conversation between people.

05

Saying so, out loud

That is why every page says “Made with AI & ♥”. Translations are drafted by AI, so if something reads oddly in your language, or anything here seems wrong or unfair, please tell me and I will fix it.
