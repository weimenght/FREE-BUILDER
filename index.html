01 / The problem

The mandatory-training sigh is expensive.

Every hour spent clicking through a deck nobody remembers is an hour of salary, attention and goodwill gone. And as AI takes over routine work, the skills that matter most — reading a room, repairing trust, really listening — are exactly the ones a slide can't teach.

02 / What we believe

People are the point.

AI and web apps are tools. What matters is what people do with each other afterwards.

01

Learn by doing — and have fun

If you can't try it, you haven't learned it. Play lowers the stakes so people dare to practise.

02

Human capabilities first

In the age of AI, empathy, curiosity and trust are the differentiators. That's where we put the practice time.

03

Learning is mutual

Everyone teaches, everyone learns. No trainees — only partners. Sharing is caring.

04

People are the end, never the means

No leaderboards ranking colleagues, no surveillance analytics. Success is a sincere conversation that wouldn't have happened otherwise.

03 / Approach

Diagnose. Build. Pass it on.

01◎

Diagnose

Listen first. A request for a “communication workshop” is often a psychological-safety or role-clarity problem in disguise.

02▦

Build

Prototype fast and test with real people. Every tool is designed to hand off — the screen sets things up, colleagues do the rest.

03⇄

Pass it on

Every programme ends with people teaching what they learned to someone else. It's the best evidence of learning — measured alongside Kirkpatrick L1–L4.
