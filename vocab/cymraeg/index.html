Sut mae'n gweithio · how it works

Modern Welsh counts in tens: dau ddeg tri is "two tens three", 23. Note dau ddeg (20, soft mutation after dau), pum deg (50) and chwe deg (60): pump and chwech shorten before a noun or deg.

Welsh is spelt the way it sounds. Learn these and you can read any word aloud.
